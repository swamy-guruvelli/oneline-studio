## AI Studio delivery direction

Use a friendly, clear, confident voice. Start with curious emphasis on “one simple sentence.” Keep the steps brisk but distinct. Pause briefly before “karaoke captions” and “one vertical video.” Finish with an inviting, practical tone.

## Narration text for TTS

```text
Can one simple sentence become a complete video? Here's the path.

Start with a topic or question. OneLine Studio turns it into a short visual explanation.

First, it checks the facts and builds a script around one useful example.

Then each part of the explanation gets a matching scene, so viewers can see what changes and why.

When the narration is ready, its words are timed to the voice. Those timings move the karaoke captions one word at a time.

Finally, the scenes, narration, and captions come together as one vertical video, ready to review and share.

Start with one sentence. Shape it into a story. Finish with a video.
```

## Instagram caption

A useful video doesn't need a complicated starting point. It can begin with one clear sentence.

OneLine Studio takes a topic or question and turns it into a short video draft. It starts by finding the one idea viewers need to understand. Then it builds a script around a concrete example, so the explanation feels grounded instead of abstract.

Next comes the visual plan. Each scene shows a different step in the idea, with motion that reveals what changes and why. The goal is to make the process easier to follow on a phone, not to fill the screen with decoration.

After the script is ready, add the narration WAV. OneLine Studio aligns the words to the voice and highlights the active word in karaoke-style captions. Then it renders the narration, animated scenes, captions, and brand mark into one vertical MP4.

That makes a simple workflow for educators, creators, and small teams: start with a prompt, shape the explanation, review the video, and share the finished clip. Keep the OneLine Studio look or swap in your own brand and visual style.

A video still needs a human review. Check the facts, timing, pronunciation, and readability before posting. The tool helps turn an idea into a draft faster. You decide what makes the final cut. Because each project stays in a simple folder, you can revisit the script, replace a scene, update the narration, and render another version without starting over.

What would you explain in one sentence? Save this workflow for your next short-form video, explainer, tutorial, or social media post.
