# Research and claim check

Checked 4 November 2026. This video describes the job-search support presented on MindKraft's own site. Product features may change, so avoid promises about outcomes or availability.

## Claims used in the narration

- MindKraft helps users explore roles that fit their experience and keep their search organised in one place. Source: [MindKraft home page](https://mindkraft.co.uk/).
- The site describes support to tailor CVs and applications, get feedback from people who understand the user's field, and track application status and next steps. Source: [MindKraft home page](https://mindkraft.co.uk/).
- A user can share a role they found and ask for help assessing its fit and preparing an application. Source: [MindKraft home page](https://mindkraft.co.uk/).
- MindKraft says users review and approve materials, and that it does not automatically submit every application. Source: [MindKraft home page](https://mindkraft.co.uk/).
- MindKraft's terms say job-search support does not guarantee interviews, offers, employment, or application success. Source: [Terms and Conditions](https://mindkraft.co.uk/terms), updated 19 July 2026.

## Caveats

- Describe support and tools as the site presents them. Do not imply guaranteed interviews, employment, or application success.
- The narration says “help” and “support”; it does not claim every feature is included for every account or plan.
- The official site is the primary source for this overview. Features can change after the verification date.
