# Visual direction

Visual language: English throughout the cover, visual beat labels, diagram labels, and other on-screen copy. The spoken narration alone is Telugu. Video subtitles are disabled.

## Central metaphor

A scattered stack of job-search tasks becomes one calm, connected application workspace. Show the user as the decision-maker at the end of the process. Use a dark technical canvas with cyan routes, amber focus points, and green reviewed/approved states.

## Beat map

Map every spoken paragraph or meaningful idea to an active visual response. The current ratios are provisional because the narration was rewritten; reset them against the replacement WAV after listening.

1. `0.00` — PROBLEM / “JOB SEARCH, SCATTERED”: separate role cards, CV pages, message alerts, and a tracking sheet drift apart. Stagger their entrances, send an amber route searching between them, and pulse the unresolved-task marker while the problem is described.
2. `0.27` — MATCH / “ROLES THAT FIT”: profile details feed a fit check. Trace the route as it moves, pulse the fit lens, then bring relevant roles into cyan or green focus while unrelated roles recede.
3. `0.53` — EXAMPLE AND PREPARATION / “TAILOR EACH APPLICATION”: the Product Designer role becomes the focus. Trace the selected role into a tailored CV and cover letter, then light the user-research example and field-aware review checkpoint in sequence.
4. `0.77` — TRACKING AND USER CONTROL / “YOU APPROVE”: update application statuses and next steps as the narration describes the dashboard. Keep the approval gate amber during review, then reveal the green user-approved state when the narration reaches the user's decision.

Each beat has a visible animated state update tied to its spoken idea. Cyan routes show work moving between steps, amber marks the current decision or attention point, and green marks a reviewed or user-approved outcome. Let each accent settle after it responds so labels remain readable. Ratios above are placeholders until the replacement WAV is heard.

The cover uses the English concept title “MindKraft: A Clearer Job Search”, `WHAT IF...?`, and the hook “Job-search support at mindkraft.co.uk”. The user interface remains English so the spoken Telugu carries the localization.

## Motion and layout

Keep the vertical frame's central diagram inside the panel with generous clear space around headings and cards. Use short English labels, with no semicolons or em dashes. Animate scattered cards into aligned lanes, then route each application through role match, tailored materials, review, and the user approval gate. Make the user's approval the clearest change. Do not add Telugu copy or subtitles to the picture. Scene starts use `startRatio` and adapt to the supplied WAV duration. Keep narration and visuals starting together and end the picture with the narration before the closing hold.
